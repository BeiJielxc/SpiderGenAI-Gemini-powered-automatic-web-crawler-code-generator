- **来源**: Deutsche Bundesbank (20884)
- **链接**: https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:share:7431756072203411456

👋 Zdravo!

A delegation of the Central Bank of Montenegro, helmed by its Governor @Irena_Radovic, visited the Bundesbank today, reaffirming the strong and long-standing cooperation between the two institutions. Talks were held with President Joachim Nagel and Executive Board Member @Burkhard_Balz on advancing Montenegro’s alignment with the European System of Central Banks (ESCB).

🇪🇺 Nagel and Radović conversed on ongoing reforms and concrete benchmarks in integrating Montenegro’s frameworks with European standards, including SEPA implementation and the development of a TIPS clone – the Eurosystem’s TARGET service for instant payments – as well as next steps in strengthening institutional and operational readiness.

During the meeting with Balz, the discussion focuseding on the modernisation of payment systems, digital innovation, and the evolving role of central banks in the digital era.

💡 The visit also highlighted the Bundesbank-led cooperation in EU-supported regional programmes, notably through the Twinning Project “Support to Regulation of Financial Services in Montenegro”. It aims to advance alignment with the EU by further strengthening the regulatory framework and supervisory practices of the Central Bank of Montenegro and the Insurance Supervision Agency.
